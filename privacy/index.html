Twisthunter

Privacy policy

Twisthunter doesn't collect anything about you.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location is used on your phone to show roads near you and follow your drive. It isn't sent anywhere.
  • Your drives, recaps, saved roads and settings stay on your phone.

What the app doesn't have

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The only thing it asks the internet

In California the app checks Caltrans' public road conditions page for closures. It sends only the road's route number, for example "SR 2". Nothing about you or your phone goes with it.

Sharing

When you share a road, a video or a route, it goes only where you choose to send it.

Purchases

Twisthunter Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or your Apple Account.

Map data

Roads come from OpenStreetMap and public state and federal road records. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page changes first, with a new date.
