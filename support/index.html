Support

Twisthunter finds the curvy roads near you and calls every curve as you drive.

Something wrong with a road, a closure, or the app? Write to us:

titef.beckham@gmail.com

Say which road (its name and the nearest town) and what you saw.

Restoring Pro

Open the Pro screen (tap Fun route) and tap Restore a purchase. Pro is a one-time purchase, so it comes back on any phone signed in to the same Apple Account, and with Family Sharing.

Using it with no signal

Roads, maps and curve calls are kept on your phone once your state is on it. They keep working where your signal drops.

Closures and gates

Many roads were checked by hand for gates, private stretches, one-way sections and seasonal closures. Roads change, though. Always follow posted signs and closures, and drive for the conditions.

Speed

Twisthunter never shows your speed, lap times or rankings, and never will.
